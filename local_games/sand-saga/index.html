The Sand Saga (formerly Sand Game JS) is a next-gen falling-sand game where players can freely experiment in the sandbox or play missions and challenges. It is also recognized by parents and teachers for its ability to develop creativity and problem-solving skills. The game is actively being developed with an update every two weeks on Sunday and a new blog post at the end of each month quarter.
